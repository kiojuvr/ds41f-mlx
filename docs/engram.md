This page owns **token-history-conditioned lookup, storage and numerical authority**.
[Session state](session-state.md) owns continuation; [runtime strategy](runtime-strategy.md)

## Production components

Engram combines tokenizer-derived normalization/compressed IDs, official integer
Ngram hashing/prime/table geometry, request-local lookback, row lookup and projection
at checkpoint-configured backbone insertions. Do not infer insertion layers from
an old example or replace official integer hashing with another RNG/hash family.

Selected OFF owners are `model_execution/{engram,storage,language,cache}.py`,
including `NgramHash`, `DiskEngramEmbedding` and `EngramPrefetch`. Their first-party
ownership retains attributed qualified algorithms and admitted primitives; it is
not an alternative checkpoint or donor-model fallback. Ordinary MTP retains its
pinned model/Engram with ds41f paired lifecycle integration. Native Engram headers,
source and Metal remain local executable reference/reusable material, not the
complete selected production engine.

## Storage policy

Production Engram tables remain SSD-backed, not canonical resident Metal tables.
Selected row gathering/dequantization/projection and bounded CPU read-ahead are
distinct from making the whole table resident. P7 overlaps SSD reads with dense
prefill under the full-resident-backbone/SSD-Engram policy. Model retirement must
drain/close owned read work and mappings; a background read is not another cache
publisher, canonical history owner or permission to reuse stale selected rows.

removing image positions from the timeline. This does not grant MTP image support;

for restoring coherent state. Pending prefetch values and HC temporaries are not
independent persistent session authority. P6 artifacts versus MTP paired capture
must preserve their own coherent lookback and layer/frontier contracts.
